Proposition 1 [Zoboli et al. 2023]
Consider system $\mathcal{S}$. Let assumption 1 hold and let $\omega(t)$ be a $\mathcal{L}_2$ function. Then, for any $\kappa>\rho/2$, the trajectories of $\mathcal{S}$ in closed loop with $$u = \psi(t) - \kappa\big(\textcolor{#598938}{\beta}(x, t) - \textcolor{#598938}{\beta}(\pi, t)\big) + \omega(t)$$ satisfy $$\Big| X\big(x(0), t_0, t) - \Pi\big(\pi(0), t_0, t)\Big| \leq k\big|x(0) - \pi(0)\big|\exp\big(-\lambda(t-t_0)\big) + \zeta\big(|\omega|\big)$$

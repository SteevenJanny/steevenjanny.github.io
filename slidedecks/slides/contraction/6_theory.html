Proposition 2 [Zoboli et al. 2023]
Consider system $\mathcal{S}$ in closed loop with the control law defined earlier. Let $(\pi, \psi)$ be the solution of the regulator equations, and $(\kappa, \beta)$ chosen as in Proposition 1. Then, for any compact sets $\mathcal{W}_{\tilde{x}} \subset \mathbb{R}^{n_x}, \mathcal{R}\subset\mathbb{R}^{n_e}$ such that $r(t)\in\mathcal{R}$ for all $t\geq t_0$ and for any $\delta\leq 0$, there exists a compact set $\mathcal{W}_x$ and scalar $\mu_\delta\leq 0$ such that, if $$\begin{array}{rl} \big|\textcolor{#598938}{\hat\beta}(x,t) - \beta(x,t)\big| &\leq \mu_\delta \\ \big|\textcolor{#598938}{\hat\pi}(t) - \pi(t)\big| &\leq \mu_\delta \\ \big|\textcolor{#598938}{\hat \psi}(t) - \psi(t)\big| &\leq \mu_\delta \end{array}$$ hold for all $x\in\mathcal{W}_x$ and for all $t\geq t_0$, then $$\lim_{t\mapsto \infty} \Big|X\big(x(0), t, t_0\big) - \Pi\big(\pi(0), t, t_0\big)\Big| < \delta$$ for any $\big(x(0), \pi(0)\big)$ satisfying $\big(x(0) - \pi(0)\big)\in\mathcal{W}_{\tilde{x}}$.
